Our Story

Wake Setters opened in 2024 in the heart of Shell Lake, Wisconsin — born from a love of good coffee, local community, and a simple belief: everyone deserves a place to slow down.

Run by the same folks behind Lakeview Bar & Grill, we've brought that same hospitality to the coffee counter. Whether you're grabbing your morning espresso, meeting a friend for ice cream, or just need a quiet corner to yourself — our door's open.

We source quality ingredients, serve Bear Lake Coffee, and make everything from scratch when we can. Swing by and see what's brewing.
